About La Reezy

From LaReezyana, with love.

La Reezy calls his home LaReezyana — his own map of New Orleans, Louisiana. He raps the way the city sounds: jazz and soul under trap drums, stories about family, grief and loyalty told with genuine positivity and hunger for greatness.

His moniker — Leader of da UTH (Utilize Time Here) — is a message to young people: lead with purpose, self-awareness and intention. In 2026 that message put him in the XXL Freshman Class and on the road with Chance the Rapper.
