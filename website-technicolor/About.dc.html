Khayree Salahuddin · 12th Ward

From LaReezyana,
with love.

He started making music at fourteen, near Napoleon Avenue. He produces and engineers his own records. At twenty-one he made the 2026 XXL Freshman Class and put out a debut that pulls jazz, soul and trap into one story about family, grief and loyalty.

He calls it rap-tivism — the music and the work are the same job.
